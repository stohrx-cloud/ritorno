The Nonna Wall

“My Nonna Maria would be thrilled to know that her picture hangs on the Nonna wall with so many remarkable women.”Julia Hanna

The Nonna wall is a small way to honour the women in our lives, our mothers and grandmothers, who fed us and nurtured us, putting their own wants and needs aside.

Have a nonna who belongs on the wall? Ask Julia next time you're in.

A return to the table, in the Italian way.

That is how Julia Hanna describes Ritorno. She grew up in an Italian home where everyone was welcome at the table, opened her first restaurant, Café Galleria in Downtown Oakville, at 26, and has spent 30 years in Oakville restaurants since.

Ritorno opened at Trafalgar and Dundas in 2008. Everything is made from scratch, the kitchen is nut-free, and the food comes out to share.

“The Italians have a gift for life.”Julia Hanna
